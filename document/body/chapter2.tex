\chapter{Literature Review} \label{chap:ch2}

This chapter investigates existing research on deploying \ac{DNN} models 
on edge and far-edge devices through a systematic literature review. We 
examine three main areas: model partitioning and split inference, \ac{ML} 
orchestration and scheduling in edge computing, and computational offloading 
for \ac{ML} inference. The chapter details the search methodology, including the
search strategy and inclusion/exclusion criteria, and 
presents the results, establishing the current state of the art in these fields.


\section{Introduction}\label{sec:se21}

This literature review followed \textcite{Kitchenham2009}'s 
Systematic Literature Review workflow.
% as outlined by Kitchenham and Charters (2007)~\cite{kitchenham2007guidelines}.
The review aimed to identify and analyze existing research relevant to the
subject of this thesis. Likewise, it was designed to provide
a strong foundation for the following topics:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Model Partition and Split Inference
    \item \ac{ML} Orchestration/Scheduling in Edge Computing
    \item Computational Offloading for \ac{ML} inference
\end{enumerate}

In addition, the proposal bibliography was also
included and examined in the context of this work.


\section{Review Methodology}\label{sec:se22}

To ensure replicability and rigor, the following subsections describe the 
methodology employed to retrieve relevant studies. Particular emphasis is 
placed on the search strategy, including the search queries used, and the 
criteria applied for filtering results.


\subsection{Search Strategy} \label{sec:se221}

There were three digital libraries used to search for relevant articles:
the IEEE Xplore Digital Library\footnote{\url{https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/Xplore/home.jsp}} and the ACM Digital
Library\footnote{\url{https://dl.acm.org/}} as the main sources of specialized
literature on the subject, and Scopus\footnote{\url{https://www.scopus.com/}} as a broader database
to cover any gaps that could arise.

To accomplish this search, the following search strings were built,
respectively for each topic:\footnote{It should be noted that the
proximity and wildcard operators were adapted to each digital library syntax.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item ("dynamic partitioning" OR "dynamic splitting" OR "model partitioning" OR "model splitting" OR "adaptive partitioning" OR "adaptive splitting") AND "inference" AND ("ML model*" OR "DNN") AND ("Edge" OR "Far-Edge")	
    \item (("resource-aware" OR "network-aware" OR "adaptive") NEAR/5 ("schedul*" OR "orchestrat*" OR "allocation")) AND ("inference" OR "ML model*" OR "DNN" OR "AI workload*") AND ("Edge" OR "IoT" OR "Far-Edge")
    \item ("inference offload*" OR "model offload*" OR "task offload*") AND ("resource-aware" OR "latency-aware" OR "adaptive" OR "resource-constrained") AND ("TinyML" OR "MCU" OR "microcontroller*")
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria} \label{sec:se222}

In order to filter the search results, the rules in Table \ref{tab:criteria} were applied to include or
exclude the documents found:

\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3} % Increases row height for better readability
    \begin{tabular}{c l p{0.65\linewidth}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Type} & \textbf{ID} & \textbf{Criteria} \\
        \midrule
        \multirow{4.5}{*}{\rotatebox{90}{Inclusion}} 
        & IC1 & The publication is in an adequate document type. \\
        & IC2 & Date of publication since 2018 (inclusive). \\
        & IC3 & The publication addresses constrained devices. \\
        & IC4 & The publication discusses network deployment or topics pertinent to it. \\
        \midrule
        \multirow{5.5}{*}{\rotatebox{90}{Exclusion}} 
        & EC1 & It is a duplicate result. \\
        & EC2 & Full-text is not available. \\
        & EC3 & The publication is not English. \\
        & EC4 & The publication does not focus on cloud computing. \\
        & EC5 & The publication does not focus on high-performance device usage. \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \caption{Inclusion and exclusion criteria for the systematic literature review.}
    \label{tab:criteria}
\end{table}

Different formats were found in the indicated set of digital libraries.
The following formats were considered adequate for this review: 
\emph{Conference Paper}, \emph{Journal} and \emph{Magazine} for \emph{IEEE Xplore};
\emph{Research-Article}, \emph{Demonstration} and \emph{Short-Paper},
%\emph{Poster} and \emph{Survey} 
for \emph{ACM Digital Library};
\emph{Article}, \emph{Conference Paper} and \emph{Conference Review},
\emph{Book Chapter}, \emph{Review} and \emph{Book} for \emph{Scopus}.
The document type filter also excluded \emph{Work-in-Progress}
documents.
