\chapter{Literary Review} \label{chap:ch2}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:se21}

The Literary Review for this work followed the standard workflow of a
Systematic Literature Review (SLR).
% as outlined by Kitchenham and Charters (2007)~\cite{kitchenham2007guidelines}.
The review aimed to identify and analyze existing research relevant to the
subject matter of this thesis. Likewise, it was designed to provide
a strong foundation on the following aspects:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Model Partition and Split Inference
    \item \ac{ML} Orchestration/Scheduling in Edge Computing
    \item Computational Offloading for \ac{ML} inference
    \item Heterogeneous Resource Management in \ac{IoT}/Edge
\end{enumerate}

In addition to these topics, the proposal's bibliography was also
included and later examined to this work's context.


\section{Review Methodology}\label{sec:se22}
\subsection{Search Strategy} \label{sec:se221}

There were three digital libraries used to search for relevant articles:
the IEEE Xplore Digital Library %insert citation
and the ACM Digital Library %insert citation
as the main sources of specialized literature on the subject, and
Scopus %insert citation
as a broader database to cover any gaps that could arise.

To accomplish this search, the following search strings were built,
respectively for each topic:\footnote{It should be noted that the
proximity operator was adapted to each digital library's syntax.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item ("split inference" OR "distributed inference" OR "split computing" OR "collaborative inference" OR "layer partitioning" OR "DNN partitioning") AND ("Edge" OR "Far-Edge" OR "TinyML" OR "microcontroller" OR "resource-constrained")	
    \item ( ("resource-aware" OR "network-aware" OR "adaptive") NEAR/5 ("schedul*" OR "orchestrat*" OR "placement" OR "allocation") ) AND ( "inference" OR "ML model*" OR "DNN" ) AND ( "Edge" OR "Fog" OR "IoT" OR "Far-Edge" )
    \item ( "inference" NEAR/3 ("offload*" OR "decision*") OR "inference offloading" ) AND ( "resource-aware" OR "latency-aware" OR "energy-aware" OR "adaptive" OR "resource-constrained" OR "decision model" ) AND ( "Edge" OR "Far-Edge" OR "IoT" OR "TinyML" OR "MCU" )	
    \item ("device heterogeneity" OR "hardware heterogeneity" OR "heterogeneous computing" OR "heterogeneous resources" OR "heterogeneous cluster") AND ("schedul*" OR "orchestrat*" OR "allocation") AND ("inference" OR "DNN" OR "ML workload*") AND ("Edge" OR "IoT" OR "Far-Edge")	
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria} \label{sec:se222}

In order to filter the search results, the following rules were applied to include or
exclude the documents found:

\hline

\hline

\hline

\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{3mm}

Different formats were found in the indicated set of digital libraries,
the following formats were considered adequate for this review: 
\emph{Conference Paper}, \emph{Journal} and \emph{Magazine} for \emph{IEEE Xplore};
\emph{Research-Article}, \emph{Demonstration} and \emph{Short-Paper},
%\emph{Poster} and \emph{Survey} 
for \emph{ACM Digital Library};
\emph{Article}, \emph{Conference Paper} and \emph{Conference Review},
\emph{Book Chapter}, \emph{Review} and \emph{Book} for \emph{Scopus}.
The document type filter was enough to also exclude \emph{work-in-progress}
documents.
