\chapter{Model Energy} \label{ap2:modelEnergy}

To provide a estimate of the energy consumption of the models throughout the network,
we have used a multimeter to measure the power consumption of each device during
inference individually. Then, taking account for each submodel in each used strategy,
we have estimated the energy consumption of each model by adding the corresponding
segments. The following sections provide a detailed overview of the energy consumption
of each model.
