\chapter{Model Specifications} \label{ap1:modelSpecs}

During the process of selecting and acquiring the models used in this dissertation,
a series of characteristics were considered, including the model's architecture, size,
and layer-by-layer information. The following sections provide a detailed overview of
the layer-by-layer details.

From Figure~\ref{fig:all-cnn-c-layers} to Figure~\ref{fig:mlp-autoencoder-layers}, 
we present the layer-by-layer information for each model, including the layer type,
input and output shapes, and output size. This information is crucial for understanding
the feasibility of running the submodels on the microcontroller, as it provides insights
into the computational requirements and memory usage of each layer.
