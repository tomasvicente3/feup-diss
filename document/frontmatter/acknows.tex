\chapter*{Acknowledgements}

This work is the crowning achievement of a long journey that I have been on for the
last five years. Throughout this time, I have had the opportunity to meet and work
with many people to whom I owe a great deal of gratitude.

First and foremost, I would like to thank Professor Filipe Sousa and Fraunhofer Portugal
for the opportunity to work on this project and for their availability to help me
embark on this project.

Recalling this last year, I would like to thank my supervisors, Fernando Rego, who has
been a great guide through this convoluted path where I started without much knowledge
about it; and Luís Almeida, who did not hesitate to give me advice whenever I had
doubts about the dissertation's specifics. As I often joked this year, "this
is my first dissertation".

Although I have had a lot on my plate, I have always had the support of my friends, who
were my family away from home. I would like to thank them for their aid in lightening
the mood and helping me get through the worries inherent to this work, always giving 
me an extra hand when I needed it.

Central to this journey has been the unwavering support of my family, who have
always been there for me, even when I was not sure about what I wanted to do with my
career. I am grateful for their unconditional support and love, which was above all
a great source of motivation for me to keep going and finish this work.

\vspace{10mm}
